AI 3300 — Statistical Learning (Spring 2026)

Fall 2026 · Ohio University

Course description:

Machine Learning (ML) studies algorithms that enable computers to learn patterns from data and make predictions or decisions without explicit programming. In this course, students will develop both the conceptual understanding and practical skills needed to build, evaluate, and interpret classical ML models. The course introduces the main paradigms of supervised and unsupervised learning, emphasizes the end-to-end ML workflow (data preparation, modeling, and evaluation). Formal concepts will be paired with hands-on examples and applications drawn from various areas.
